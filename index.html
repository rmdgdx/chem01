Course Outcomes

By the end of the course, students should be able to:

CO1 · ENERGY

Explain chemical energy systems

Explain how chemical reactions produce, store, or transform energy, including electrochemical and nuclear processes.

CO2 · MATERIALS

Connect structure with performance

Relate atomic structure, bonding, and material structure to the properties and uses of engineering materials.

CO3 · ENVIRONMENT

Evaluate environmental processes

Explain chemical processes in air, water, and soil and evaluate basic pollution-prevention and control approaches.

CO4 · APPLICATION

Solve engineering-context problems

Apply chemistry concepts, calculations, evidence, and safety practices to a selected engineering field.

Course Requirements

  • Pass the preliminary, midterm, and final examinations.
  • Complete quizzes, worksheets, problem sets, class work, and application tasks.
  • Submit the field-specific mini-project or presentation.
  • Maintain and submit a complete e-portfolio.
  • Participate in recitation, review, discussion, and peer feedback.
  • Demonstrate responsible data use, chemical safety awareness, and academic honesty.
